Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Lvl Up Button XP (“the Game”, package com.nickdev.lvlupbuttonxp) handles information. The Game is developed by Nikita Ryasnianskyi (NickDev) (“we”, “us”).

1. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally on your device: current XP, number of rewarded ads watched, unlocked achievements, and the sound mute setting. This information is not sent to our servers and is not linked to your identity. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling the Game.

2. Advertising

The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation and the advertising networks connected to it. These ad partners may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, screen size);
  • ad impressions, clicks and related diagnostics.

This data is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve advertising performance. It may be shared with ad networks and partners as described in their privacy policies. For more information, see the Unity privacy policy at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy and the LevelPlay documentation.

3. No account, no sign-in

The Game does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

5. Data deletion

Game progress is stored only on your device. To delete it, clear the Game's data in Android settings or uninstall the Game. For questions about data processed by ad partners, contact us using the email below.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
